**Theorem A.**
In the finite subgame with (X,Y\subset[0,1]), let (x_{\min}=\min X), (y_{\min}=\min Y), the profile ((x_{\min},y_{\min})) is a pure Nash equilibrium if

1. (x_{\min}=y_{\min}), or
2. (x_{\min}=\tan(y_{\min})).

**Proof.**
Players best respond by minimizing the distance to $y_min$ and $\arctan(x_min)$ respectively.
In either case, one of the players is playing a best response already, while the other is playing the closest allowed value:
as (1.) $\arctan(x_min) \le y_min = min Y$; (2.) $y_min \le x_min = min X$.


**Theorem.**
All sequences of subgames generated by DO eventually fall into one of the cases of theorem A if initialized at single point.

**Proof.**
The situation is trivial for any initial point on x=y, or x_init=tan y_init. For other cases, players add the actions BR_x(y_init)=y_init, and BR_y(x_init)=atan(x_init).
1) for any point x_init < y_init, after adding best responses, the minimal elements are x_init, atan(x_init), respectively, satisfying case 2.
2) for any point y_init < arctan(x_init), after adding best responses, the minimal elements are y_init, y_init, respectively, satisfying case 1.
